# Nomenclature

| Symbol / term | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| φ | nominal shiftable fraction: share of evening cooking events a household moves into the afternoon under a ToU discount | – |
| φ_eff | effective shiftable fraction applied in a scenario (φ under ToU with discount > 0, else 0) | – |
| d | daytime (09:00–16:00) ToU discount | US$/kWh |
| p | eCooking penetration: share of households with an electric pressure cooker | – |
| N | households (= connections) in the village archetype, 300 | – |
| DF | diversity factor: N × appliance rating / peak of the average daily profile (MECS definition) | – |
| GHI | global horizontal irradiance (plane-of-array proxy) | W/m² |
| P_PV | PV capacity | kWp |
| E_B | nominal battery capacity | kWh |
| P_inv | inverter rating, sized to design-year peak load × 1.25 | kW |
| η_rt | battery round-trip efficiency, 0.90 | – |
| DoD | depth of discharge, 0.80 | – |
| u_y | unmet demand fraction in project year y; constraint max_y u_y ≤ 0.05 | – |
| g | annual demand growth, 0.05 | yr⁻¹ |
| r | real discount rate (swept 8–20%) | yr⁻¹ |
| LCOE | levelised cost of energy, gross CAPEX + discounted OPEX and replacement over discounted energy served | US$/kWh |
| NPV, IRR | net present value, internal rate of return of the operator's cash flows | US$, – |
| CAPEX, OPEX | capital, operating expenditure (OPEX per customer per year) | US$, US$/yr |
| fNRB | fraction of non-renewable biomass | – |
| MTF | ESMAP Multi-Tier Framework household survey | – |
| RAMP | open-source stochastic appliance load model (Lombardi et al., 2019) | – |
| EPC | electric pressure cooker, 1 kW | – |
| ToU | time-of-use tariff | – |
| RURA, EPRA | Rwanda / Kenya energy regulators | – |
| RBF | results-based financing | – |
| LST | local solar time | – |
